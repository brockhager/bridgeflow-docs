Using BridgeFlow — Quick Guide for End Users

This guide is for end users who will use BridgeFlow in their job. You do not need to install or run anything — use the web app your organization provides.

Where to access the app - Ask your BridgeFlow administrator for the web app link and your login credentials. If you don't know who that is, ask your manager or IT help desk.

Common tasks (simple steps) - Create a Trading Partner 1. Open the Trading Partners page. 2. Click New (or Add Trading Partner). 3. Fill the form with the information you have (name, contact, basic settings) and click Save.

  • Activate a Blueprint (turns a blueprint into a working package)
  • Open Blueprints, find the blueprint you need, and click Activate.
  • Fill any requested details and confirm.

  • The activation runs in the background. You'll see a status like Provisioning → Active. You can leave the page and check back later.

  • Check status or troubleshooting

  • Open the Packages or Activations page to see the current state and basic diagnostics.
  • If something shows an error, take a screenshot of the status/details and the time you tried it.

If you don't have permission - If you see a permission error or an action is unavailable, contact your BridgeFlow admin. Tell them what you were trying to do and share any screenshots.

Simple tips - Do not share admin credentials. Only give screenshots or details to the support person handling your issue. - Activation may take a few minutes; wait a little and refresh the package status.

Need help or want to learn more - Glossary (terms and names): docs/glossary/README.md - Ask your BridgeFlow admin or open a support ticket with the screenshot and a brief description of the problem.
