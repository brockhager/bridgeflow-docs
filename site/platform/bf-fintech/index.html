Fintech Service (bf-fintech)¶

Freight billing and payment workflows, including invoice retrieval and payment webhooks.

Status¶

Development.

Endpoint¶

  • Stable public base endpoint not documented yet.

Key Routes¶

  • GET /health
  • GET /invoices
  • GET /invoices/{invoice_id}
  • POST /invoices/{invoice_id}/pay
  • POST /webhooks/stripe
