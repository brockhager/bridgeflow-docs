TRACKING PORTAL¶

bf-tracking-portal is the customer/public shipment tracking experience.

Search Entry Options¶

  • Track by shipment ID.
  • Track by email + order reference lookup.

Tracking Detail Experience¶

  • Shipment status and ETA visibility.
  • Risk badge derived from anomaly status checks.
  • Timeline component for event progression.
  • Carrier contact display.
  • Notification preference controls.

UX Characteristics¶

  • Dedicated public entry page and shipment-specific route.
  • Loading and invalid-shipment error states.
