Layer 1 Blueprints: Empowering Business Users in Integration

What is a Layer 1 Blueprint?

Layer 1 Blueprints are pre-packaged Integration Templates designed specifically for business users. Unlike traditional integration scripts that require coding, a Blueprint defines the business intent of a workflow without exposing the complex technical details underneath.

Imagine a Recipe Card for your digital ecosystem: - It knows the Goal: "Sync Shopify Orders to Quickbooks" - It knows the Ingredients: Order Data, Customer Details, Tax Codes - It knows the Steps: Validation -> Transformation -> Creation

Why Use Blueprints?

For non-IT users (Business Analysts, Operations Managers, Partner Managers), Blueprints offer a way to deploy sophisticated integrations in minutes, not months.

  • No Code Required: You don't need to write SQL, JavaScript, or understand API protocols.
  • Embedded Best Practices: Each blueprint is built by experts to handle errors, retries, and data security automatically.
  • Standardization: Ensure every integration follows your company's approved processes.

How to Use a Blueprint

Using a Layer 1 Blueprint is as simple as filling out a form.

Step 1: Browse the Catalog

Navigate to the Blueprint Library in BridgeFlow. You can filter by: - Category: E-commerce, Logistics, ERP, Finance - Apps: Shopify, NetSuite, Salesforce, EDI - Goal: "Order to Cash", "Inventory Sync"

Step 2: Select & Preview

Click on a card to see what the blueprint does. It will show you: - Visual Flow: A simple diagram of the data movement. - Requirements: Which systems you need access to (e.g., "Requires a generic Email account").

Step 3: "Fill in the Gaps"

When you click "Use this Blueprint", BridgeFlow creates a new Bridge and asks you to configure only the specific parts that change for your scenario. We call this "Gap Filling".

You might be asked to: 1. Select Connections: Choose your specific Shopify store and your specific ERP login from a dropdown. 2. Set Parameters: "Sync orders from the last 30 days" or "Default warehouse location: NY-01". 3. Map Custom Fields: If your business uses unique data (like "Order Color"), you can map it using a simple drag-and-drop interface.

Step 4: Validate & Activate

Once configured, you can run a Test Transaction with one click. BridgeFlow will verify that: - Your connections are working. - The data is flowing correctly. - No errors are generated.

If everything looks green, hit Activate, and your integration is live!

Summary for Business Leaders

Traditional Integration Layer 1 Blueprints
Requires dedicated IT developers Self-service for Ops/Business teams
Weeks/Months to build Minutes to configure

Layer 1 Blueprints turn integration from a technical bottleneck into a business accelerator.
